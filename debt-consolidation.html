Mortgage Debt Consolidation Calculator

Notes for completion & how to use

This calculator is intended for use once Steps 1–3 of the debt consolidation assessment process have been completed, and you are engaging in a discussion with the customer regarding debt consolidation options.

It enables you to:

  • Calculate the costs of adding your customer's debt(s) to their mortgage
  • Compare this cost to their current situation
  • Analyse the suitability of adding unsecured debts to the mortgage
  • Provide a quality recommendation so the customer can make an informed decision
Before you start, you will need:
• The Cost of £1 over the term of the mortgage — found in Section 3 of the ESIS/KFI document for the recommended mortgage.
• Details of each unsecured debt: amount outstanding, monthly payment, term remaining, interest rate, and total interest repayable.
• Use the CC Calculator tab to calculate total interest payable on credit cards or loans where you don't have this figure to hand.

1 Entering Debt Details

Start on the Debt 1 tab and work through numerically. Set the number of debts using the selector at the top of the page.

Important — enter debts in term order, shortest first. If your customer has three debts, enter the shortest-term debt on Debt 1, the medium-term debt on Debt 2, and the longest-term debt on Debt 3.

Enter the Cost of £1 over the mortgage term once in the settings card at the top — it applies automatically to all debts.

  • Complete all yellow/input fields for each debt
  • The consolidation analysis (mortgage repayment, interest via consolidation, overall increase) is calculated automatically as you type
  • The Net Interest Payable Until End of Term must be entered manually — use the CC Calculator tab if you need to work this out

2 Suitability Assessment (per debt)

At the bottom of each debt tab, select Yes or No in the 'Suitable for Recommendation?' box.

  • Select Yes if the debt is being consolidated into the mortgage — the debt will then appear in the Total Debts summary
  • Select No if consolidation is not recommended for this specific debt
  • Complete the Reasoning box to document your rationale in both cases

Only debts marked as 'Yes' will be included in the Total Debts page.

3 Total Debts Page

If you have identified more than one debt suitable for consolidation, use the Total Debts tab to assess the combined position.

The Total Debts page shows the combined outstanding balance, monthly payments, net interest, and the overall increase in interest across all debts marked as suitable. Use this to make your final overall suitability assessment.

Complete the Overall Conclusion box with your final recommendation before exporting.

4 Presenting Your Advice & Exporting

Once you have completed your assessment and formulated your recommendation, use the Export PDF or Export Excel buttons on the Total Debts tab.

  • Export PDF — produces a branded client-facing document with all debt details, calculations, reasoning, and the combined totals summary. Suitable for inclusion in a suitability report.
  • Export Excel — produces a workbook with a summary sheet and one tab per debt, useful for file records or further analysis.

The Total Debts comparison can also be used as part of your presentation to the customer where you feel the recommendation would benefit from being illustrated this way — but only use where relevant.

CC Calculator tab: Use this to calculate the total interest payable on a credit card or loan balance before entering the 'Net Interest Payable Until End of Term' figure into a debt tab. Enter the balance, APR, and monthly repayment and the calculator will show months to repay, total repayable, and total interest.
